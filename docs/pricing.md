as a zero line. Charging for those separately reads badly to a client.

developer band. The out of scope hourly is deliberately higher at 250, so
extra requests cost the client more than planned work does.

## Your open questions, answered

**What is the base worth?** 2,800. Around half of a store only project, which
leaves room for the parts that actually vary between clients.

**Per page or bundled?** Per page at 450. Offer 4 pages for 1,500 when someone
wants a fuller site, so the bundle is visibly the better buy.

**Is there an hourly rate?** Only for work outside an agreed quote, at 250.
Never open a project on hourly. Fixed scope, fixed price, extras billed.

**Do integrations cost the same?** No. See that section.

**Base versus separate?** Included: mobile, testing, sitemap and robots,
product upload to 15, pre launch checks, handoff. Everything else is billed.

## The thing this catalogue exposed

Price the full package scope out of this sheet and it comes to about 16,650:

base 2,800, custom product page 900, cart 700, upsell 700, payment 650,
invoicing 450, shipping 550, domain 350, hosting 400, business email 300,
SSL 300, SEO 700, analytics 350, pixels 850, six creatives 1,500, first
campaign 1,200, homepage design 1,400, product page design 750, brand
identity 1,800.

You are selling that for 7,500.

À la carte should run maybe 1.4 times the bundle, because the bundle is the
reward for buying everything. It should not run 2.2 times. Something has to
give, and it is not the line items, because those are already priced at the
bottom of the Israeli market.

Two honest options:

1. **Move עיצוב מותג out of the full package** and sell it as an add on. That
   removes 1,800 of scope in one move, and plenty of clients already have a
   logo.
2. **Raise the full package** to about 9,500 launch and 12,500 real, which
   lands the bundle at roughly 1.4 times à la carte where it belongs.

Do both and the numbers line up cleanly.

This is the same point from earlier in a harder form. I said the risk was
scope, not price. Your own catalogue just proved it: the site promises
fourteen deliverables and the price assumes about eight of them. Fix the
scope on the site or fix the number, but they cannot both stay as they are.

You also cannot deliver that full scope in 30 hours. Brand identity, two
designed page types, four integrations, six creatives and a campaign launch
is a 55 to 65 hour project. Worth knowing before you promise four weeks.
